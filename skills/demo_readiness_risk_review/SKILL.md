# Demo Readiness Risk Review

## Purpose

Use this skill when you need to decide whether a project is good enough for recruiting demos, interviews, or short live presentations.

## Required Inputs

- Demo or interview context
- Main risk points
- What needs to be proven

## Operating Procedure

1. Clarify the audience and time budget.
2. Identify the top failure modes.
3. Check whether the project has visible evidence, trace, and evaluation.
4. Keep the story short enough to tell live.
5. End with a demo script and fallback plan.

## Output Contract

- Demo readiness summary
- Risk table
- Evidence gaps
- Backup narrative
- Interview Q&A notes
